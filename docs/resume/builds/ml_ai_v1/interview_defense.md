## cv_pneumonia

Expect. Is 0.959 a clinical result?
Answer. No. It is a Kaggle public score for a Harvard BST-261 course competition.

Expect. What was the 14-point gap?
Answer. 99 percent-plus validation versus 85 percent leaderboard, traced to memorization of about 4,700 training images.

Expect. Why did freeze vs fine-tune beat architecture?
Answer. Bank key finding. DenseNet-121 95.6 percent. ResNet-50 93.8 percent. The larger move was 85 percent to 95 percent-plus from training recipe, not from swapping backbones.
