# Prepare a coffee chat

Using the person's verified profile details and the target job:

1. Explain why this person is relevant.
2. Draft a short personalized outreach message.
3. Prepare five questions that cannot be answered by a basic company search.
4. Avoid asking for a referral in the opening message.
5. After the conversation, produce structured notes and a follow-up action for `data/networking.csv`.
